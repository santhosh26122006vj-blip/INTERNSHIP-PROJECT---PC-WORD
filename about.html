About PC Word

Built by people who build PCs

What we do

PC Word is a hardware store for anyone assembling their own computer — from a first budget build to a maxed-out workstation. We keep the catalog focused: five well-chosen options per category, so picking parts doesn't mean scrolling through hundreds of near-identical listings.

Every product page shows the specs that actually matter for compatibility and performance, and checkout is a single page — no accounts required to browse, no surprise steps at the end.

What we carry

Processors, graphics cards, memory, storage, cooling, monitors, keyboards & mice, and cabinets — the eight categories that make up a complete build, all in one place.
